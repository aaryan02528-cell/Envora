## ESP32-C3 Hardware Verification

### Initial Issue
During initial bench setup, the ESP32-C3 development board did not behave correctly when powered, preventing reliable firmware deployment and serial output execution.

### Investigation & Resolution
1. The development board hardware and pin soldering connections were inspected under physical examination with guidance from the project supervisor.
2. Cold joints / improper soldering and connection issues were identified and resoldered/corrected.
3. Upon reconnection to USB power, the board successfully entered the bootloader, accepted firmware flashes, and continuously executed a baseline test script outputting `"ESP32-C3 alive..."` over the Serial Monitor at `115200` baud.

---

## DHT11 Sensor Integration

Following baseline MCU verification, the DHT11 environmental sensor module was connected to the ESP32-C3:

- **Signal (S)** $\rightarrow$ **GPIO 3**
- **VCC (+)** $\rightarrow$ **3.3V**
- **GND (-)** $\rightarrow$ **GND**

Dedicated test firmware [`dht11_test.ino`](file:///C:/Users/aarya/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/Envora/firmware/src/dht11_test/dht11_test.ino) was deployed. The sensor successfully initialized and returned consistent periodic telemetry:
- Temperature: $26.5\text{–}26.9\ ^\circ\text{C}$
- Humidity: $75\%\text{ RH}$

## Engineering Learning
This session highlighted the importance of physical hardware verification prior to diagnosing firmware bugs. Verifying soldering integrity, supply voltages, and continuity avoids chasing non-existent code issues.

Envora enforces a strict **incremental fault-isolation engineering methodology**:

$$\text{ESP32 Alone} \longrightarrow \text{Sensor Integration} \longrightarrow \text{Display Interface} \longrightarrow \text{Complete System} \longrightarrow \text{Standalone Power}$$
